# 🌌 Cosmos World Model Prompts

These prompts are used with NVIDIA Cosmos foundation models to generate diverse and realistic environments, lighting conditions, and edge cases to ensure the vision model is robust.

## 1. Factory Lighting Variations

- `An industrial automotive paint shop inspection tunnel with flickering fluorescent lights, high contrast, casting moving shadows on a central object.`
- `A factory floor with uneven overhead LED lighting, alternating bright spots and dark shadowy areas on a metallic surface.`
- `An inspection station lit by a single, high-intensity halogen spot lamp from a 45-degree angle, creating harsh specular highlights.`
- `A manufacturing plant interior during a power brownout, dim ambient lighting with emergency yellow LEDs active.`

## 2. Reflective Surface Scenarios

- `A highly polished curved metallic surface reflecting the complex geometric structure of a factory ceiling crane.`
- `A glossy black automotive panel reflecting a person walking past wearing a bright yellow high-visibility jacket.`
- `A wet-look clear coat surface acting like a mirror, reflecting multiple harsh pinpoint light sources causing severe lens glare.`
- `A metallic silver fender reflecting the distorted grid pattern of an overhead light box.`

## 3. Edge-Case Scenarios

- `A painted surface showing signs of high humidity, with a very fine, barely visible layer of condensation misting the gloss finish.`
- `A partially cured painted panel, showing slight tackiness and uneven light reflection compared to a fully dried surface.`
- `A painted metal sheet passing through a thermal curing oven, showing slight heat haze and shimmering air above the surface.`
- `A dusty factory environment where airborne particulate matter is settling onto a freshly painted, static-charged surface.`

## 4. Environment Generation

- `A robotic inspection cell with a quadruped robot and robotic arm positioned near an automotive chassis.`
- `An end-of-line quality control station in a modern automotive plant, featuring stark white walls and clean epoxy floors.`
- `A heavy machinery manufacturing bay, gritty and industrial, with sparks flying in the distant background.`
